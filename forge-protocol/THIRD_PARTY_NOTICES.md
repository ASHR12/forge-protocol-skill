## The Gauntlet Loop

Matt Shumer, https://somethingbig.ai/gauntlet-loop. Credited as the source of the overall method: goal over implementation, a concrete bar, agent-chosen decomposition into separately judged pieces, a separate builder and fresh critic for each piece, looping past the first decent result, a live progress page, and wave smoothing passes.
